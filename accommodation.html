Hotels

The Hyatt-Regency in Columbus' Short North District is the venue and main hotel for the 2008 conference.

There are group rates available to conference participants: $140.00 per night for a single occupancy room. Shared rooms are available, for $140+ $10 per night for each extra person, up to a total of four people in the room. Four people sharing would be $42.50 a night each. Hyatt Regency ACL 2008 Group Booking Page. The special rate is available for the nights of 14-20 June inclusive. Update: Hyatt is basically sold out for nights of 19 and 20 June. A few rooms are available for some of the other days.

Hyatt Regency Columbus

Columbus, Ohio, USA 43215
Tel: +1 614 463 1234 Fax: +1 614 280 3034

We also have a block of rooms reserved at the Red Roof Inn, literally across the street from the Hyatt Regency. The group rate there is accessible by phoning the reservation line at 1-800-733-7663 and is available from 14-20 June. Please quote the group number B262ACL08. Similar deals are available on multiple occupancy as at the Hyatt, but the base room rates should be much lower.

If you are hoping to share a room, but need us to find you a room mate, please fill out a roommate request.

If you are unable to find space in either of the conference hotels, please contact us and we will do what we can to find you something. There are plenty of hotels in Columbus, including some others that are close to the conference venue and/or reasonably priced.
